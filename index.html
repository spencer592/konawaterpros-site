Why filtered water, and why not just a pitcher

Pitchers and refrigerator filters are mostly designed for chlorine taste and odor, plus whichever contaminants are listed for that specific cartridge. They are usually not reverse osmosis, so do not assume they reduce fluoride, nitrate, sodium or dissolved solids.

Reverse osmosis membranes are extremely fine, with openings far smaller than most microplastic particles. That is why RO is often discussed as a way to reduce them, though results for any specific system depend on its own testing.

Whatever you choose, look for certified contaminant claims, and replace filters on schedule.
